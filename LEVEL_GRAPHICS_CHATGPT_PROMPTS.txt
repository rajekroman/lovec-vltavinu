LOVEC VLTAVÍNŮ: ZELENÁ VLNA — PROMPTY PRO VYGENEROVÁNÍ LEVELŮ

ÚČEL
Tyto obrázky budou použity jako terrain plates / hlavní kulisy ve webové hře
s ortografickou kamerou. ChatGPT má vytvořit čtyři samostatné statické obrazy
prostředí. Do výsledků se budou až ve hře doplňovat pohyblivý hráč, NPC,
interakční ikony, HUD, joystick, akční tlačítko, marker nálezu a hazardy.

SPOLEČNÝ VIZUÁLNÍ SMĚR — POUŽIJ U VŠECH ČTYŘ PROMPTŮ

- Stylizovaná, velmi detailní low-poly / ručně malovaná 3D dioráma české
  krajiny. Má působit jako kvalitní herní screenshot, nikoliv jako fotografie.
- Jedna ortografická kamera shora pod mírným šikmým úhlem (isometric-like),
  přehledná kompozice pro mobilní hru.
- Vysoká hustota drobných, ale čitelných detailů: kamínky, nerovnosti,
  vegetace, cesty, stopy po práci a přirozené okraje prostředí.
- Výrazné tvary, cinematic ambient occlusion, měkké směrové světlo,
  matné materiály, jemná malovaná textura. Žádný fotorealismus.
- Jižní Čechy, nikoliv fantasy. Žádné hory, palmy, anglické silnice,
  sci-fi, středověké ruiny ani generický fantasy les.
- Nech kompozičně čitelný volný průchod přes střed mapy a několik širších
  bezpečných ploch pro 2D postavy. Důležité oblasti nesmí být zakryté stromy.
- Žádný text, nápisy, logo, UI, HUD, health bar, joystick, tlačítko,
  kurzor, postava hráče, NPC, vltavínový sparkle, interakční kruh, šipka
  ani watermark. Žádné vyříznuté postavy.
- Bez rámečku; obraz vyplní celé plátno až do krajů.
- Output: landscape PNG, co nejvyšší rozlišení, ostrý a použitelný jako
  game background. Zachovej přibližný poměr stran uvedený u levelu.

1. CHLUM — POLE PO DEŠTI
====================================================================

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full level terrain plate for an orthographic mobile game
Reference image: supplied screenshot is a reference for camera angle, detail
density, wet furrows, South Bohemian agricultural atmosphere and composition
quality only; do not copy its HUD, characters or UI.

Primary request:
Create a highly detailed stylized low-poly orthographic game environment of a
freshly ploughed South Bohemian field after rain. The image must immediately
read as "Chlum: wet field and tractor country".

Scene/backdrop:
A broad dark-brown field with long, clearly visible wet furrows running
diagonally across the map. Muddy water glints in shallow parallel puddles.
The foreground has a muddy farm track, grass verge, small stones, yellow and
white field flowers, fence posts and hedges. The upper edge opens into a
layered South Bohemian village landscape: hedgerows, meadow bands, scattered
trees, red-roofed houses and a small distant church with a tower under a soft
overcast sky. This distant landscape is part of the diorama background, not a
real horizon panorama.

Composition/framing:
Orthographic top-down three-quarter view. Keep a wide navigable central and
right-hand section of the field. Put dense shrubs, field edges and a wooden
fence around the lower and side boundaries. Include two hay bales as static
landmarks near field edges, but do NOT include a tractor because the game
spawns a moving tractor separately. Keep an uncluttered safe patch near the
lower-middle for the player spawn and a clear open patch on the right-middle
for the search site. Place no object at these two patches.

Lighting/mood:
Soft cloudy late-afternoon South Bohemian light, wet earth highlights,
restrained warm light beyond the field, calm but slightly dangerous farm work
atmosphere.

Color palette:
Deep umber wet soil, charcoal furrows, olive and fresh grass greens,
muted straw gold, slate-gray sky, small warm red roofs in the distant village.

Materials/textures:
Chunky low-poly mud clods, wet ruts, puddles, irregular stones, faceted bushes
and trees; detail level comparable to a polished premium mobile adventure.

Constraints:
1600:1200 composition (4:3 landscape). No text. No HUD. No player. No NPC.
No tractor. No marker. No sparkle. No icon. No watermark.

2. NESMĚŇ — LESNÍ VLTAVÍNOVÁ LOKALITA
====================================================================

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full level terrain plate for an orthographic mobile game

Primary request:
Create a highly detailed stylized low-poly orthographic game environment of a
South Bohemian mixed forest used for respectful moldavite searching. The image
must immediately read as "Nesměň: deep green forest, forest path and old soil
profiles".

Scene/backdrop:
A dense but readable spruce-and-deciduous forest, with groups of mature trees,
faceted dark green canopies, mossy rocks, ferns, exposed roots, fallen branches
and damp leaf litter. A natural brown forest path curves from the lower-left
entrance through several small clearings toward the upper-right. Three visibly
different old shallow excavation / soil-profile areas sit beside the route:
exposed pale sandy soil, dark forest earth, small mound edges, stones and
carefully restored ground. They must look like local field research spots, not
fantasy ruins and not deep mines.

Composition/framing:
Orthographic top-down three-quarter view. Build clear sight lines and broad
passages between tree clusters. Keep a small guard/forester clearing in the
upper-left with a simple wood pile, stump and modest forest-management feel.
Make three objective clearings easy to distinguish: one near centre-left, one
in the broad centre, one in the upper-right. Keep each clearing open enough
for an overlay NPC and a player. Do not place people, signs or dig markers.

Lighting/mood:
Cool filtered daylight through canopy, small shafts of soft warm light on the
three clearings, moist and secretive but never horror-like.

Color palette:
Layered spruce green, moss green, damp brown, charcoal bark, pale sand and
subtle muted teal shadows.

Materials/textures:
High density of readable low-poly foliage, clustered rather than randomly
scattered trees, mossy stone facets, wet leaf-litter ground, visible roots.

Constraints:
1500:1200 composition (5:4 landscape). No text, HUD, player, NPC, vltavín,
sparkle, interaction circles, arrows, fantasy props, mining machinery or
watermark.

3. BESEDNICE — TVRDÁ PŘEKOPANÁ LOKALITA
====================================================================

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full level terrain plate for an orthographic mobile game

Primary request:
Create a highly detailed stylized low-poly orthographic game environment of
Besednice, the toughest moldavite locality: a scarred South Bohemian clay and
stone excavation landscape. It must immediately read as "intensively dug,
rocky, exposed soil and valuable geological profile".

Scene/backdrop:
An irregular bowl-like old excavation in warm ochre, brown and clay-red earth.
Layered exposed soil profiles, low earth embankments, stone piles, old shallow
pits, loose rocks, rutted clay paths and sparse scrub occupy the scene.
Vegetation survives only at outer edges: low bushes, a few small trees and
grass on the rim. The central terrain must look dangerous and worked over,
but it must still provide readable mobile-scale routes.

Composition/framing:
Orthographic top-down three-quarter view. Arrange the map as progressive
revelation: a safer lower-left entry shelf; an easy first clue area left of
centre; a second open clue plateau at centre; a third clue shelf in the
upper-right; a visually dominant distant geological "hedgehog" profile at the
north-east edge; and a broad unobstructed confrontation arena in the south-east.
Keep all six areas visibly connected through generous clay paths. Use embankments
and rock clusters as edges, not as a maze. No human characters, markers, gems
or active digging tools.

Lighting/mood:
Harder contrast than Chlum and Nesměň, late warm sun with long directional
shadows inside the excavation, tense expedition mood without becoming fantasy.

Color palette:
Clay orange, ochre, raw umber, slate gray rock, dusty beige and restrained
scrub green.

Materials/textures:
Faceted layered cut faces, chunky low-poly rocks, loose gravel, soil strata,
irregular pits and wheel-rutted dry clay. Strong spatial depth but no vertical
cliffs that hide the player.

Constraints:
1680:1280 composition (21:16 landscape). No text, HUD, player, NPC, Karel,
vltavín, sparkle, icon, weapon, fantasy cave, giant mine machinery or watermark.

4. KD SLAVIA — NA ZELENÉ VLNĚ
====================================================================

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full level terrain plate for an orthographic mobile game

Primary request:
Create a highly detailed stylized low-poly orthographic game environment of
the final destination: a South Bohemian cultural-house plaza for a moldavite
event called Na Zelené Vlně. It must immediately read as "arrival at a public
mineralogical event, urban finale after the countryside".

Scene/backdrop:
A recognisable cream and warm sandstone cultural-house facade occupies the
right side and upper-right, with a broad dark entrance canopy and a plaza in
front. The left and centre are a clean paved arrival forecourt bordered by
city trees, lamp posts, low railings, benches and a short street approach.
Add several tasteful exhibition tents, wooden display tables, glass-vitrine
silhouettes, banner stands with blank fabric (absolutely no readable text),
visitors implied only by empty infrastructure — no people. The atmosphere is
an evening event with warm architectural lighting contrasting cool blue city
shadows.

Composition/framing:
Orthographic top-down three-quarter view. Keep an open arrival route from the
lower-left to a clear registration zone in the centre-left, then through an
open event plaza to the cultural-house entrance on the right. Leave distinct
clear pads for three document pickup points, an expert/porota position and a
final doorway interaction. The building must be a dominant landmark without
hiding the playable route. Do not render any text onto banners or the building.

Lighting/mood:
Blue-hour city ambience, amber window and lamp glow, celebratory but grounded
small-town Czech cultural event.

Color palette:
Warm cream facade, muted sandstone paving, deep blue shadow, amber lights,
dark green urban trees and restrained moldavite green accent cloth.

Materials/textures:
Low-poly facade rhythm, arched entrance, paving tiles, glass display cases,
fabric tents, wooden tables, simple lamps and faceted urban trees.

Constraints:
1800:1100 composition (18:11 wide landscape). No readable text, UI, HUD,
player, NPC, judges, visitors, logo, watermark, floating gems, buttons or
interaction markers.

JEDNOTNÝ ZÁVĚREČNÝ PŘÍKAZ PRO CHATGPT
====================================================================

For every output, preserve the same camera elevation, low-poly hand-painted
material language, object scale, shadow softness and mobile-game readability.
Generate one level per image. Do not collage the four localities into one
picture. Do not include any words or game interface. The result must work as
a clean environment layer with gameplay sprites and HTML HUD added afterwards.
